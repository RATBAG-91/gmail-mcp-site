gmail-mcp

gmail-mcp is a private tool that helps its owner organise their own Gmail inbox using Claude, an AI assistant. It runs only on the owner's personal computer.

It can manage Gmail filters and labels, archive or label groups of messages, move messages to Trash, summarise the inbox, and read the owner's email attachments. It cannot send, reply to or forward email, and it never permanently deletes anything.

It is not offered to the public and has a single user.
