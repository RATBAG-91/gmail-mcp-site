gmail-mcp privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

gmail-mcp is a private tool used by one person to organise their own Gmail account. This policy explains what it does with Gmail data.

Who can use it

Only the owner. It is not offered to anyone else, and it is configured to refuse any Google account other than the owner's.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, granted through Google's sign-in screen, it reads the owner's email messages, labels, filters and attachments, and changes labels and filters. It uses two Google permissions: gmail.modify and gmail.settings.basic.

Where data goes

The tool runs entirely on the owner's own computer. Gmail data is passed only to the owner's Claude desktop app on that computer, at the owner's request. It is not sold, shared with, or sent to any other person or service, and it is not used for advertising.

What is stored

On the owner's computer only: the Google sign-in token, a log of changes made, a cache of sender and unsubscribe headers, and any attachments the owner chooses to download. The owner can delete these files at any time.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account's third-party connections page.

Limited Use

gmail-mcp's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
